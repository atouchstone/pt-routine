Ankle Routine

Current routine from Dr. Bui · Nov 13, 2026 visit

Every day · ~12 min

Strength day (Mon/Wed/Fri) · +8 min

Keep it pain-free. Stop short of the front-of-ankle pinch. Ball work is also great right before a run.

Running (Dr. Bui, 11/13/26): Wait until 4-6 weeks (or Dec 1) to try running. First week of running should be ~20 minutes total of jogging, broken up into walk-run intervals. Should be pain free. If you have some stiffness or discomfort at the beginning of the run and it subsides after the warm up, most likely coming from the peroneal tendon. Tenderness along the outer leg should resolve.
